## Movie Story Summaries

### 1. Avengers: Endgame
*After Thanos wipes out half of all life in the universe, the remaining Avengers struggle to move forward. They eventually work together to find a way to reverse what happened and bring back those they lost.*

### 2. Avengers: Infinity War
*Thanos searches for all six Infinity Stones to gain control over the universe. The Avengers and the Guardians of the Galaxy join forces to stop him before he can complete his mission.*

### 3. Doctor Strange
*After a serious accident damages his hands, talented surgeon Stephen Strange searches for a way to recover. His journey leads him to discover the mystical arts and become a powerful sorcerer.*

### 4. The Avengers
*When Loki threatens Earth with an alien invasion, Nick Fury gathers a team of superheroes. Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye must work together to protect the world.*

### 5. Captain America: Civil War
*The Avengers become divided over a government agreement that would control their activities. Captain America and Iron Man disagree about what is right, causing a conflict between two groups of heroes.*

### 6. Iron Man Trilogy
*The trilogy follows Tony Stark, a billionaire inventor who becomes Iron Man after building a powerful suit of armor. Throughout the three movies, he faces dangerous enemies, personal challenges, and learns what it truly means to be a hero.*

### 7. Black Panther
*After the death of his father, T'Challa returns to Wakanda to become king. He must protect his kingdom and face a challenger who questions how Wakanda should use its power and resources.*
